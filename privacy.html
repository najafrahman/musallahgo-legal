Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Musallah Finder helps people find places to pray. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our login provider; we never see it). You can browse as a guest without any of this.
  • Your location: when you allow it, the app sends your approximate position to our server to find prayer spaces near you. We do not store your location with your account. When you open a place we record only a coarse distance band (here, walk, drive or far), never coordinates.
  • Content you add: forum posts and replies, reactions, check-ins, saved places and labels, place suggestions, reports, feedback, and listings and photos if you host a space. If you ask to manage a masjid, the contact details and proof you provide in the claim.
  • Notification settings and your phone's push token, so we can send the reminders you choose.
  • Basic technical details sent with feedback: app version and phone platform.

Anonymous posts

You can post or reply anonymously. Other people never see who wrote it. We still keep the link between an anonymous post and the account that wrote it, so moderators can act on abuse and so you can delete your own posts.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show nearby places, save your favourites, post to the community and send notifications you asked for.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove abusive content and suspend accounts that break the Terms.
  • To improve the app and fix problems, using the feedback you send.
  • We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who helps us run the app

These providers process data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: our database, login and photo storage.
  • Expo, Apple and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): app updates and delivery of push notifications.
  • Mapbox: the map. Mapbox may collect anonymous usage and device information to provide and improve maps under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors: much of the place data comes from OpenStreetMap, which is open data and not about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, saved places, check-ins, posts, replies, reactions, notification settings and push tokens. Anonymous feedback and reports you sent may be kept without a link to you. Listings you created stay available to the community without an owner unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and its data any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account.
  • Change or turn off notifications, and hide your name by posting anonymously.
  • Turn off location access in your phone's settings; the app still works with a search.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion at najafrahman@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have additional rights, and you can contact us to use them.

Children

Musallah Finder is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and our database enforces access rules so people can only read and change what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact
