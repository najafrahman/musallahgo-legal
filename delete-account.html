Delete your account

Last updated 20 October 2026

You can delete your Musallah Finder account and its data at any time.

In the app

Open Profile, tap Delete account and confirm. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email najafrahman@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject Delete my account. We will delete it and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, name and email
  • Saved places, labels and check-ins
  • Forum posts, replies, reactions and blocks
  • Notification settings and push tokens

What may remain

Feedback and reports you sent are kept without a link to you. Listings you created stay available to the community without an owner unless you ask us to remove them.
